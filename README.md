# 🌡️ SIH26083 – Extreme Heatwave Early Warning System

**Team ClimateResilience**

An Extreme Heatwave Early Warning and Thermal Stress Dashboard developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Problem Statement SIH26083.

The platform aims to identify heatwave risks, assess human thermal stress, and provide early warnings with personalized safety recommendations.

## 🌐 Live Demo

[Visit Heatwave Early Warning Dashboard](https://sih-heatwave-projection.vercel.app/)

## ✨ Key Features

- **Ward-Level Risk Monitoring:** View heatwave risk across different municipal wards.
- **Heatwave Risk Assessment:** Monitor temperature, NOAA Heat Index, Outdoor WBGT, and UTCI.
- **Personal Heat Twin:** Calculate personalized heat exposure risk based on age, occupation, activity, and location.
- **Profession-Based Risk Analysis:** Specialized risk assessment for students, delivery workers, construction workers, farmers, and elderly people.
- **5-Day Predictive Risk Trend:** Explore upcoming heat risk trends.
- **SMS & WhatsApp Alerts:** Interface for subscribing to heatwave warning notifications.
- **Historical Backtesting:** Explore historical heatwave conditions, including the May 2010 Ahmedabad heatwave.
- **Vulnerability Analysis:** Consider elderly populations, outdoor workers, informal housing, and green cover.
- **Heat Copilot:** Conversational assistant for heat safety information and dashboard guidance.
- **Emergency Safety Recommendations:** Provides preventive measures and heat-related safety advice.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop an intelligent heatwave early warning platform that helps people and authorities understand extreme heat conditions.

It combines meteorological information with human vulnerability indicators to identify high-risk areas and provide timely safety recommendations.

## 🏙️ Pilot Location

Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC), Gujarat, India.

The platform includes ward-level monitoring across 48 municipal wards.
